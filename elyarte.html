Biography

Frida Kahlo is one of the most striking figures in the art of the twentieth century. Despite the terrible accident that changed her whole life, Frida managed to find herself, become one of the best artists in the world and maintain her fortitude until the last days.

Creativity

Lying in bed, recovering from a serious accident, Frida began to paint the first pictures. Since then, themes of death and the fragility of the human body have often appeared in her paintings. And the hospital bed became a certain symbol of her creativity.
